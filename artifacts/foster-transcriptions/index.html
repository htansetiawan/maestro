Experiment 029 · recordings read by ear

Foster recordings, transcribed

Twenty-four tracks from two David Foster albums, transcribed from the audio by the ByteDance piano transcription model (Kong et al., 2021): the notes it found, the first thirty seconds of each transcription played back by one fixed piano soundfont, engraved where the piano carries the arrangement, and the MIDI and MusicXML of that excerpt.

The model listens for a piano. These records are full productions, so strings, synthesizers and voices come back as piano notes too, and the counts include them; the solo-piano passages are where the transcriptions are closest to what was played. The playback keeps each performance's own timing and dynamics, because they come from the recording; the sustain pedal the model found is in the MIDI but not in the playback, which renders notes only. The MusicXML is a rough score: notes snapped to sixteenths on the beat grid tracked from the recording (so bar lines follow the player's tempo, with the first note starting bar 1 and 4/4 assumed), split into two staves at middle C with one chord per onset, in the key estimated from the notes themselves; enough to read, not an engraving. Where the tracker finds the eighth-note pulse, bars come out at half their printed length. Everything on this page is an excerpt (the first thirty seconds); the counts were measured on the whole tracks. The recordings themselves are not on this page. Engravings are shown only for the tracks where the piano carries the arrangement (The Colour of My Love, Water Fountain, Jelinda's Theme, Ice Dance, Glory of Love, Small Hands Reach Out, Allegro); on the others, voices, drums and strings come back as piano notes and the score is not worth reading, though the playback and files are kept.
