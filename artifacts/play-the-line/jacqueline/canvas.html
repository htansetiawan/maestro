Jacqueline

Bars 1 to 16. Click any note to hear the piano and the voice from there. Piano: David Benoit, Jacqueline, his own recording, a short research excerpt. Composition by David Benoit. The piece is an instrumental. The sung line is the melody David Benoit plays, read phrase by phrase from a transcription of the recording, an octave down. The words are not his: they were written for this line by the agent as placeholders, one syllable per note. He plays freely in time, so the bar lines were placed by hand on the bass notes that start each bar. Each pill names whose recording the AI voice's sound was copied from and the key it sings in; for mine the piano is moved up four semitones and I sing an octave down. Every voice here is an AI singer: SoulX sings from the score, copying the sound of a short reference clip, short excerpts of published recordings by Josh Groban and Phil Perry, and my own song The Yard; Josh Groban and Phil Perry did not sing these takes. The voices are not the singers and are not endorsed by them. If you represent one of these artists and want a clip removed, open an issue on the project's GitHub and it will be taken down.
