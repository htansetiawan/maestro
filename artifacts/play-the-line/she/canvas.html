Bars 3 to 15. Click any note to hear the piano and the voice from there. Piano: the play-along Notting Hill OST _ She - Piano Accompaniment by allmusicsheet.com on YouTube, a short research excerpt. Song by Charles Aznavour and Herbert Kretzmer; the words are the song's. The voice line is the song's, read bar by bar from a voice-and-piano score and moved up a half step to D, the play-along's key. Each pill names whose recording the AI voice's sound was copied from and the key it sings in; for C and B♭ the piano is moved down with it. Every voice here is an AI singer: SoulX sings from the score, copying the sound of a short reference clip, a short excerpt of a published Josh Groban recording; Josh Groban did not sing this take. The voices are not the singers and are not endorsed by them. If you represent one of these artists and want a clip removed, open an issue on the project's GitHub and it will be taken down.
