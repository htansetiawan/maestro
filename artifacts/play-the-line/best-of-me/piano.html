How this was made

  • The voice was separated from the recording (SoulX-Singer's preprocessing). What is left is the whole band: keyboards, guitar, bass, drums and Dave Koz's saxophone. A piano-only separator found almost no acoustic piano under the verse, so the whole band was transcribed.
  • Kong et al.'s high-resolution piano transcription turned the band into notes. Notes above C6 (mostly cymbal and saxophone overtones) and very short or quiet notes were dropped: 471 notes remain in these 16 bars. Read it as a rough band reduction, not a piano score.
  • Each note is placed on the measured beat (F♯ major, 4/4, about 53 beats a minute, with live rubato), snapped to sixteenths. Bar 1 starts on "years" in "So many years gone"; notes from middle C up are in the right hand.
  • The tune is not in the band: a melody pulled from this transcription matched at most 13% of Phil Perry's sung notes. The melody comes next, played in by hand on the recorder.
