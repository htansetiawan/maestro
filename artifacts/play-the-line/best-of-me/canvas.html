The Best of Me

Click any note to hear the band and the voice from there. Band: Dave Koz & Phil Perry, The Best of Me, live at the Strand, with Phil Perry's voice separated out; a short research excerpt. Composition by David Foster, Jeremy Lubbock and Richard Marx. The melody I played on the recorder over that band, an octave down where the singer sings it; the words are the song's, in F major, a half step below the recording. Every voice here is an AI singer: SoulX sings from the score. Each pill names whose recording the sound was copied from and the key the song was moved to for that singer (the band moves with it). The sources are short excerpts of published recordings by the named singers (Phil Perry's from this same live recording) and Eidee's own recordings as a teenager, used with Eidee's agreement; those singers did not sing this take.
