Music by Frédéric Chopin, arranged for violin and piano by Nathan Milstein. In this arrangement the violin carries the tune. It was taken out of the recording by separating the instruments and cutting the violin's pitches, so faint traces remain. The sung line is the violin's tune, read from its pitch track and sung an octave down, with its runs and trills left out. The words are not Chopin's: the agent wrote them for this line as a draft, one syllable per note. He plays freely in time, so the bar lines were placed by hand on the bass notes; bar 7 is where the violin enters. Each pill names whose recording the AI voice's sound was copied from and the key it sings in; for Josh Groban and Bryan Adams the piano is moved down four semitones, and I sing an octave down. Every voice here is an AI singer: SoulX sings from the score, copying the sound of a short reference clip, short excerpts of published recordings by Josh Groban and Bryan Adams, and my own song The Yard; Josh Groban and Bryan Adams did not sing these takes. The voices are not the singers and are not endorsed by them. If you represent one of these artists and want a clip removed, open an issue on the project's GitHub and it will be taken down.
