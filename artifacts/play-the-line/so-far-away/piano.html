How this was made

  • The voice was separated from the recording (SoulX-Singer's preprocessing), then the piano from what was left (an open band-split RoFormer piano model).
  • Kong et al.'s high-resolution piano transcription turned the piano into notes: 430 in these 16 bars.
  • Each note is placed on the measured beat (D major, 4/4, about 64 beats a minute), snapped to sixteenths; half the attacks moved 5 ms or less. Notes from middle C up are in the right hand. In each hand a note is shown ending at that hand's next attack, so a note held under moving ones reads shorter than it sounds.
  • The tune is not in the piano: she plays chords and fills under her own voice. A melody pulled from this transcription matched 12% of her sung notes, so the melody was played in by hand on the recorder; it is on the score canvas.
