## Investigation planned

Two input paths need separate tests:

```text
PDF/image → score reading → MusicXML → checked musical events → ABC
Sound/humming → transcription → checked musical events → ABC/MusicXML
```

First check whether the notes, rhythms and rests survive conversion. For piano scores, track the melody, left hand, chord tones and bass separately. Then test simple melody imitation and an isolated vocal before adding accompaniment.

The earlier Color Purple trial used a manual melody/chord reduction, not the full piano arrangement. The documented YuE2 input workflow uses two monophonic lines plus chord symbols; that does not preserve every written piano note. Whether it follows even the retained melody in the audio remains the test.

These are prepared experiments and plans, not new quality results. The separate October 19 draft remains unfinished.
