How the thinking connects

The research map organizes prior work. The evidence notes distinguish reported capabilities from hypotheses. The weekly journal records decisions, contradictions, and changes of direction instead of silently replacing the past.

Questions to build through

How can a musician correct accompaniment by humming or tapping? What should remain fixed when one phrase changes? Can examples convey a desired feel without forcing it into words? Research follows these product questions. Planned experiments and their eventual results belong in the journal.
