MAESTRO · EXPERIMENT 016

From a scanned song sheet
to a singer.

She (Aznavour and Kretzmer), a three-page piano-vocal score. Three OMR engines read the vocal staff; a manual transcription of the same staff (195 printed notes, 33 bars, both lyric lines) is the reference. The transcribed line, its chord symbols and the printed lyrics then go to YuE2 as a complete lead sheet, and the singer's pitch is tracked back against the line it was given. The same tracker recovers 71% of the synthesized guide (repeated pitches merge into one segment), so 71% is the ceiling of this measurement, not 100%.

Reference: the vocal line as transcribed

Deterministic synth of the linearized song (intro, two verses, bridge, third verse, coda). Not a model output.

Global pitch alignment of the engine's first-part note sequence against the transcribed vocal line. All three engines misread the opening bars of each verse in the same way, collapsing the descending sequence (E5, C5, A4) onto its middle bar; the bridge and coda were read almost perfectly by homr. No engine returned the chord symbols or the lyrics; those were typed from the sheet.
