MAESTRO · EXPERIMENT 013

Can software read the sheet
before a model plays it?

Last week's score-conditioned YuE2 run started from a hand-made reduction of the five scanned pages of Theme from The Color Purple. This page checks the first link: three optical music recognition engines read the same scan, and their readings are compared with the manual lead line (298 notes, 88 measures, 98 chord symbols). The scanned pages are copyrighted and stay offline.

Reference: the manual lead line

Experiment 007's melody guide, deterministic synth.

Recall and precision come from a global alignment of the engine's right-hand top line against the manual melody, so a missed or extra measure costs notes rather than breaking the comparison. "Measures identical" counts bars whose top-line onsets and pitches match exactly; it is only computed when the engine found the printed number of measures on every page.
