and they approve retaining/exporting lossless audio.

  MP3 conversion and validation. Never delete user-supplied source recordings.
- Keep small prompts, lyrics, settings, provenance and validation records.
- Do not rewrite Git history or delete older experiment artifacts as an implied
  part of this preference; retrospective cleanup needs explicit scope.
