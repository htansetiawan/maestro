Four ways to tell YuE2 what to play.

The pickup, intro and first theme of Theme from The Color Purple (measures 0–16, F major, 88 BPM, 44 s as written). YuE2's score interface accepts two single-line voices and quoted chord symbols, so the printed left hand and chord voicings can never be sent as notes. These runs vary only what the score carries; the style prompt (solo piano, no vocals) and the empty lyrics are shared. One seed per condition, two for the 007 recipe. Four of the five clips ran to the token cap instead of ending where the score ends.

How adherence is measured, and the ceiling

Each clip is transcribed to notes with the ByteDance piano transcription model, the highest note at each attack is taken as the top line, and that sequence is aligned globally to the written melody. Melody recall is the share of written notes found in order (any octave); written octave requires the register on the page; top-line precision is the share of top-line attacks that belong to the melody, which is low even for a real pianist because accompaniment attacks also reach the top line; the longest exact run counts consecutive written notes reproduced in order and octave; tempo is the fitted time stretch of the matched notes relative to 88 BPM. The first 60 seconds of each clip is the fair window, since the written excerpt lasts 44 seconds and the clips run on.
