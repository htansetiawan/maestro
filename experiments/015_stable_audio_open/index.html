Stable Audio Open 1.0 is a text-to-audio latent diffusion model (47 s, 44.1 kHz stereo) with no score input. Two questions: what does it produce for the piano prompt used in the YuE2 trials, and how much of the original recording survives when the recording is used as the starting point (audio-to-audio at increasing noise levels), including the LEGO-style request to add an orchestra.

How survival is measured

Chroma similarity is the mean frame-wise cosine between the 12-bin pitch-class profiles of the source and the output (1.0 = identical harmony over time). The top-line numbers reuse experiment 014's method: both clips are transcribed to notes, the highest note at each attack is aligned, and recall says how many of the source's top-line notes reappear. These grade note content, not sound quality.
